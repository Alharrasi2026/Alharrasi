Al-Harrasi Ropes Factory is a proud branch of Al-Amoudi International Group and the first rope manufacturing facility in the Sultanate of Oman to offer 100% Omani-made products.

Our Story

From Vision
to Reality

In 2018, a bold vision became reality in Muscat's Amrat Industrial Area. Al-Harrasi Ropes Factory was founded with a singular purpose: to manufacture world-class ropes, entirely within the Sultanate of Oman.

Backed by cutting-edge machinery, a skilled Omani workforce, and an uncompromising focus on quality, we quickly emerged as a key player in the regional rope manufacturing sector.

Today, we produce a wide range of products — from polypropylene and raffia to braided, cotton, and nylon varieties — serving agriculture, marine, construction, and industrial clients across Oman and the Gulf region.
